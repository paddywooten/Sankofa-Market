## 2. How the Escrow Service Works

### 2.1 Overview

The Escrow Service is a payment protection mechanism that holds funds securely until both parties fulfill their obligations in a transaction. This ensures:

- **Buyers** receive the item as described before payment is released
- **Sellers** are guaranteed payment once the buyer confirms delivery

### 2.2 Transaction Flow

1. **Purchase:** Buyer pays for an item via Mobile Money, bank card, or other supported payment method
2. **Escrow Hold:** Funds are held securely by Sankofa Market (not sent directly to seller)
3. **Delivery:** Seller ships or delivers the item to the buyer
4. **Inspection Period:** Buyer has 48 hours to inspect the item
5. **Confirmation:** Buyer confirms the item matches the listing, OR raises a dispute
6. **Release/Refund:**
   - If confirmed: Funds are released to seller (minus platform commission)
   - If disputed: Funds remain in escrow until dispute resolution
   - If no action: Funds auto-release to seller after 48 hours

### 2.3 Commission

Sankofa Market charges a commission fee (currently 5%) on each successful transaction. This fee is deducted from the seller's payout.

**Example:**
- Item price: GHS 1,000
- Commission (5%): GHS 50
- Seller receives: GHS 950

## 5. Dispute Resolution

### 5.1 Raising a Dispute

Either party may raise a dispute within 48 hours of delivery (or expected delivery date if item not received).

**Valid dispute reasons:**
- Item not received
- Item not as described
- Defective or damaged item
- Wrong item sent
- Significant discrepancy from listing

### 5.2 Evidence Requirements

**Buyers must provide:**
- Photos of received item
- Photos of packaging (if damaged)
- Detailed description of the issue
- Comparison with original listing

**Sellers must provide:**
- Proof of shipment (tracking number, receipt)
- Photos of item before shipping
- Delivery confirmation
- Any communication with buyer

### 5.3 Resolution Process

1. **Dispute Filed:** Both parties notified, funds remain in escrow
2. **Evidence Collection:** Both parties submit evidence within 48 hours
3. **Admin Review:** Sankofa Market admin reviews evidence (24-48 hours)
4. **Decision:** Admin makes binding decision:
   - **Refund to Buyer:** Full refund, seller gets item back (buyer pays return shipping)
   - **Release to Seller:** Funds released, buyer keeps item
   - **Partial Refund:** Split funds based on circumstances
   - **Request More Evidence:** Extend timeline for additional information

### 5.4 Appeals

Decisions are final and binding. However, if new evidence emerges within 7 days, either party may request a review by contacting support@sankofamarket.com.

### 5.5 Timeline

- Dispute filing: Within 48 hours of delivery
- Evidence submission: 48 hours from dispute filing
- Admin decision: 24-48 hours from evidence deadline
- Fund transfer: Within 3 business days of decision

## 6. Auto-Release Policy

### 6.1 Automatic Release

If the buyer does not confirm delivery OR raise a dispute within 48 hours of delivery confirmation, funds will be automatically released to the seller.

### 6.2 Exceptions

Auto-release will NOT occur if:
- A dispute has been raised
- The buyer has contacted support about an issue
- There are suspicious activity flags on the transaction
- The seller has been reported for fraud

### 6.3 Buyer Responsibility

Buyers are responsible for:
- Tracking their orders
- Inspecting items promptly
- Taking action within the 48-hour window

Failure to act within 48 hours constitutes acceptance of the item.

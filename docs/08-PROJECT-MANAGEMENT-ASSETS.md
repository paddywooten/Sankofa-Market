## 1. INTRODUCTION

### 1.1 Purpose
This document provides a comprehensive collection of project management assets, templates, and tools used throughout the Sankofa Market Ghana project lifecycle. It serves as a reference for project managers and team members to ensure consistent project management practices.

### 1.2 Scope
This document covers:
- Project charter and initiation documents
- Project planning templates
- Execution and monitoring tools
- Risk management assets
- Communication management templates
- Quality management tools
- Change management procedures
- Project closure documents

### 1.3 Project Management Methodology

The Sankofa Market project follows a **hybrid methodology** combining:
- **Waterfall:** For well-defined phases (initiation, planning, closure)
- **Agile:** For development and iterative delivery
- **Critical Path:** For schedule management
- **Earned Value:** For performance measurement

### 5.2 Risk Assessment Matrix

```
RISK ASSESSMENT MATRIX
======================

PROBABILITY SCALE
-----------------
5 - Almost Certain: >90% chance of occurring
4 - Likely: 60-90% chance of occurring
3 - Possible: 40-60% chance of occurring
2 - Unlikely: 10-40% chance of occurring
1 - Rare: <10% chance of occurring

IMPACT SCALE
------------
5 - Catastrophic: Project failure, >50% budget overrun
4 - Major: Major delays, 25-50% budget overrun
3 - Moderate: Significant delays, 10-25% budget overrun
2 - Minor: Minor delays, 5-10% budget overrun
1 - Negligible: Minimal impact, <5% budget overrun

RISK SCORING MATRIX
-------------------
        Impact →    1      2      3      4      5
                  Negligible Minor  Moderate Major  Catastrophic
P  5 Almost      5      10     15     20     25
r  Certain
o  4 Likely      4      8      12     16     20

a  2 Unlikely    2      4      6      8      10
b  1 Rare        1      2      3      4      5
i
l
i

y
↓

RISK LEVELS
-----------
HIGH (15-25): Requires immediate attention and active management
MEDIUM (8-14): Requires regular monitoring and mitigation planning
LOW (1-7): Monitor and document, minimal action required

RISK RESPONSE STRATEGIES
-------------------------
AVOID: Eliminate the threat by changing project plan
MITIGATE: Reduce probability and/or impact
TRANSFER: Shift impact to third party (insurance, outsourcing)
ACCEPT: Acknowledge risk and prepare contingency plan

For opportunities:
EXPLOIT: Ensure opportunity is realized
ENHANCE: Increase probability and/or impact
SHARE: Allocate ownership to third party
ACCEPT: Willing to take advantage if it arises
```

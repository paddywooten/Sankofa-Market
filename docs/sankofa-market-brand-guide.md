## 🔤 TYPOGRAPHY

### **Primary Font: Poppins**
**Usage:** Headings, titles, brand name

**Weights:**
- **Bold (700):** Main headings, brand name
- **Semi-Bold (600):** Subheadings, buttons
- **Medium (500):** Emphasis, navigation

**Example:**
```

Give Your Items New Life (Semi-Bold 600)
```

### **Secondary Font: Inter**
**Usage:** Body text, descriptions, UI elements

**Weights:**
- **Regular (400):** Body text, descriptions
- **Medium (500):** Emphasis, labels
- **Light (300):** Subtle text, captions

**Example:**
```
Browse thousands of pre-loved items from trusted sellers across Ghana. (Regular 400)
```

### **Typography Hierarchy**

**H1 (Main Heading):** Poppins Bold 700, 3rem  
**H2 (Section Heading):** Poppins Semi-Bold 600, 2.5rem  
**H3 (Subheading):** Poppins Medium 500, 2rem  
**H4 (Card Title):** Poppins Medium 500, 1.5rem  
**Body Text:** Inter Regular 400, 1rem  
**Small Text:** Inter Light 300, 0.875rem

## 📐 LOGO USAGE GUIDELINES

### **Clear Space**
Always maintain clear space around the logo equal to the height of the "S" in "Sankofa". This ensures the logo stands out and remains legible.

### **Minimum Size**
- **Print:** Minimum 1 inch (2.54 cm) wide
- **Digital:** Minimum 100 pixels wide

### **Logo Variations**

**Full Color (Primary)**
- Blue-black text with gold accents
- Use on white or light backgrounds

**Monochrome (Dark)**
- All blue-black
- Use on light backgrounds

**Monochrome (Light)**
- All white
- Use on dark backgrounds

**Reversed**
- White logo on blue-black background
- Use for dark backgrounds, merchandise

### **Incorrect Usage**
❌ Don't stretch or distort the logo  
❌ Don't change the colors  
❌ Don't rotate the logo  
❌ Don't add effects (shadows, gradients)  
❌ Don't place on busy backgrounds  
❌ Don't use minimum size or smaller

## 🎯 BRAND STORY

### **The Sankofa Philosophy**

The Sankofa bird is one of the most recognized Adinkra symbols in Ghana. It depicts a bird looking backward while moving forward, carrying an egg in its mouth. This powerful symbol teaches us:

**"Se wo were fi na wosankofa a yenkyi"**  
*"It is not wrong to go back for that which you have forgotten."*

### **Our Brand Story**

At Sankofa Market, we embrace the Sankofa philosophy. We believe that every item has a story, and every pre-loved item deserves a new chapter.

**Our Mission:**
To create Ghana's most trusted marketplace where pre-loved items find new homes, promoting sustainability, community, and the wisdom of retrieving value from the past.

**Our Vision:**
To become Africa's leading second-hand marketplace, connecting communities and giving items new life, one transaction at a time.

**Our Values:**
- **Sustainability:** Promoting reuse and circular economy
- **Community:** Connecting Ghanaians through trade
- **Trust:** Building a safe and reliable marketplace
- **Cultural Pride:** Celebrating Ghanaian heritage
- **Innovation:** Using technology to simplify trading

## 🎨 DESIGNER NOTES

### **Logo Design Rationale**

**Concept 1 (Modern Sankofa Bird):**
I chose a modern, minimalist approach to make the logo memorable and scalable. The stylized bird represents the core philosophy of Sankofa - retrieving value from the past. The geometric design ensures it works well as an app icon and across all digital platforms.

**Concept 2 (Traditional Adinkra Symbol):**
This concept honors the authentic Sankofa Adinkra symbol, which is deeply rooted in Ghanaian culture. The circular design represents community and continuity. This concept is perfect for cultural campaigns and official brand identity.

**Concept 3 (Text + Symbol):**
This is the most versatile option, combining the brand name with the Sankofa bird icon. It's professional, established, and works everywhere - from website headers to business cards. This is my top recommendation for the primary brand logo.

### **Color Choice Rationale**

**Blue-Black (#1a237e):**
I chose blue-black as the primary color because it represents trust, professionalism, and reliability - essential qualities for a marketplace. It's also modern and sophisticated, appealing to a wide audience.

**Gold (#d4af37):**
Gold represents Ghana's rich heritage (the Gold Coast), premium quality, value, and success. It's the perfect accent color that adds warmth and prestige to the brand.

**White and Gray:**
These neutral colors provide balance and ensure the brand colors stand out. They're professional, clean, and work well for backgrounds and text.

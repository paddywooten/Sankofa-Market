## 1. INTRODUCTION

### 1.1 Purpose
This document outlines the comprehensive training and knowledge transfer strategy for the Sankofa Market Ghana platform. It ensures that all stakeholders have the knowledge and skills necessary to effectively use, manage, and support the platform.

### 1.2 Scope
This document covers:
- Training needs analysis
- Training program design
- Training delivery methods
- Knowledge management strategy
- Documentation and resources
- Training evaluation and metrics
- Ongoing learning and development

### 1.3 Training Philosophy

Our training approach is guided by these principles:

**User-Centered Learning:**
- Training tailored to user roles and needs
- Practical, hands-on learning experiences
- Real-world scenarios and examples
- Immediate application of knowledge

**Blended Learning Approach:**
- Mix of learning methods (online, in-person, self-paced)
- Multiple formats (videos, guides, workshops)
- Flexible learning paths
- Continuous learning opportunities

**Knowledge Sharing Culture:**
- Encourage knowledge sharing across teams
- Document lessons learned
- Create communities of practice
- Foster mentorship and coaching

**Measurable Outcomes:**
- Clear learning objectives
- Measurable competency levels
- Regular assessment and feedback
- Continuous improvement

## 4. KNOWLEDGE MANAGEMENT

### 4.1 Knowledge Management Strategy

#### Knowledge Types

**Explicit Knowledge:**
- **Definition:** Documented, codified knowledge
- **Examples:** Procedures, manuals, guidelines, best practices
- **Storage:** Knowledge base, documentation repository
- **Access:** Searchable, organized, version-controlled

**Tacit Knowledge:**
- **Definition:** Personal, experiential knowledge
- **Examples:** Insights, intuitions, skills, expertise
- **Storage:** People's minds, experiences
- **Access:** Mentorship, communities of practice, storytelling

**Implicit Knowledge:**
- **Definition:** Knowledge that can be articulated but isn't documented
- **Examples:** Unwritten rules, informal practices, tribal knowledge
- **Storage:** Team culture, informal networks
- **Access:** Observation, participation, inquiry

#### Knowledge Lifecycle

**Creation:**
- Identify knowledge needs
- Capture knowledge from experts
- Document explicit knowledge
- Encourage knowledge sharing
- Validate knowledge quality

**Organization:**

**Distribution:**

- Facilitate knowledge access

**Evolution:**

## 5. TRAINING EVALUATION

### 5.1 Evaluation Framework

#### Kirkpatrick Model

**Level 1: Reaction**
- **What:** Measure participant satisfaction
- **How:** Post-training surveys, feedback forms
- **Metrics:** Satisfaction ratings, engagement levels
- **Questions:**
  - Was the training relevant?
  - Was the training engaging?
  - Was the instructor effective?
  - Were the materials useful?
  - Would you recommend this training?

**Level 2: Learning**
- **What:** Measure knowledge and skills acquired
- **How:** Quizzes, tests, assessments
- **Metrics:** Test scores, skill demonstrations
- **Questions:**
  - Did participants learn the content?
  - Can participants demonstrate skills?
  - Did participants achieve learning objectives?
  - How much did participants improve?

**Level 3: Behavior**
- **What:** Measure application of learning on the job
- **How:** Observations, performance reviews, surveys
- **Metrics:** Behavior changes, performance improvements
- **Questions:**
  - Are participants applying what they learned?
  - Has their performance improved?
  - Are they using new skills and knowledge?
  - What barriers exist to application?

**Level 4: Results**
- **What:** Measure business impact of training
- **How:** Business metrics, KPIs, ROI analysis
- **Metrics:** Productivity, quality, customer satisfaction, revenue
- **Questions:**
  - Did training improve business results?
  - What is the ROI of training?
  - How did training contribute to goals?
  - What is the long-term impact?

### 5.2 Evaluation Methods

#### Surveys and Questionnaires

**Post-Training Surveys:**
- **Timing:** Immediately after training
- **Format:** Online survey
- **Content:** Satisfaction, learning, instructor, materials
- **Analysis:** Quantitative and qualitative

**Follow-Up Surveys:**
- **Timing:** 30-90 days after training
- **Format:** Online survey
- **Content:** Application, behavior change, impact
- **Analysis:** Trend analysis, correlation analysis

**Pulse Surveys:**
- **Timing:** Regular intervals (monthly, quarterly)
- **Format:** Short online survey
- **Content:** Quick feedback on specific topics
- **Analysis:** Trend analysis, sentiment analysis

#### Assessments and Tests

**Knowledge Tests:**
- **Format:** Multiple choice, true/false, short answer
- **Timing:** Before and after training
- **Content:** Factual knowledge, concepts
- **Scoring:** Automated scoring, benchmarking

**Skills Assessments:**
- **Format:** Practical exercises, simulations
- **Timing:** During and after training
- **Content:** Skill demonstration, application
- **Scoring:** Rubric-based scoring, expert evaluation

**Certification Exams:**
- **Format:** Comprehensive exam
- **Timing:** After completing learning path
- **Content:** All learning objectives
- **Scoring:** Passing score required, certification awarded

#### Observations and Reviews

**Performance Observations:**
- **Method:** Direct observation of work
- **Timing:** 30-90 days after training
- **Focus:** Application of learned skills
- **Documentation:** Observation checklist, notes

**Performance Reviews:**
- **Method:** Manager review of performance
- **Timing:** Regular performance review cycle
- **Focus:** Overall performance improvement
- **Documentation:** Performance review form, goals

**Peer Reviews:**
- **Method:** Colleagues review each other
- **Timing:** After collaborative work
- **Focus:** Teamwork, collaboration, skills
- **Documentation:** Peer review form, feedback

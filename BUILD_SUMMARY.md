## 🎯 SANKOFA STORE FEATURES

### **What is Sankofa Store?**
Sankofa Store is your **official in-house marketplace** where you can sell products directly to customers, alongside the C2C marketplace where users sell to each other.

### **Benefits:**
- ✅ **Official Badge**: Special "Sankofa Store" badge on all your products
- ✅ **Priority Placement**: Your products appear first in search results
- ✅ **Trust Factor**: Customers trust official store products more
- ✅ **Revenue Stream**: Sell your own products alongside user listings
- ✅ **Brand Control**: Complete control over your product listings
- ✅ **Analytics**: Track sales and performance separately

### **How It Works:**
1. Products marked with `isSankofaStore: true` in the database
2. Special badge appears on product cards
3. Filter option to show only Sankofa Store products
4. Priority placement in search results
5. Separate analytics for store vs marketplace

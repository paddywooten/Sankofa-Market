Buyer Guide

Your complete guide to shopping safely and confidently on Sankofa Market. Learn how to find great deals, avoid scams, and make the most of your shopping experience.

How Buying Works

1

Browse & Discover

Search for products using the search bar, browse by category, or check out featured deals. Use filters to narrow down by price, location, condition, and seller rating. Don't forget to check the Sankofa Store for curated, quality-guaranteed products!

2

Research the Seller

Before buying, check the seller's profile. Look for verified badges, read their reviews from other buyers, and check their response rate. Sellers with high ratings (4+ stars) and many completed transactions are generally more reliable.

3

Ask Questions

Not sure about something? Message the seller directly through our chat system. Ask about product condition, dimensions, delivery options, or anything else. A good seller will respond promptly and honestly.

4

Make a Secure Payment

Add items to your cart and proceed to checkout. Pay securely using Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo Money) or card via Paystack. Your payment is held in escrow — the seller only receives it after you confirm delivery.

5

Receive & Confirm Delivery

Track your order from your dashboard. When the item arrives, inspect it carefully before confirming delivery. If everything looks good, confirm delivery to release payment to the seller. Leave a review to help other buyers!

Shopping Safely

Never Pay Outside the Platform

Our escrow system protects your money. If a seller asks you to pay directly via bank transfer or Mobile Money outside Sankofa Market, refuse and report them. We cannot protect transactions made outside the platform.

Inspect Before Confirming Delivery

When you receive your item, open it and check it carefully before confirming delivery. Once confirmed, the payment is released to the seller and disputes become harder to resolve.

Read Reviews

Check both product reviews and seller reviews before purchasing. Look for patterns — if multiple buyers report the same issue, be cautious. Pay attention to recent reviews as they're most relevant.

Report Suspicious Activity

If something seems too good to be true, it probably is. Report suspicious listings, sellers who pressure you, or anyone asking for personal information. Use the "Report" button on any product or seller profile.

Watch for Red Flags

Be wary of: prices that are unrealistically low, sellers with no reviews, blurry or stock photos, sellers who refuse to answer questions, and anyone who asks to meet in private locations.

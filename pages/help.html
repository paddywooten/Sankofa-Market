Getting Started

What is Sankofa Market?

Sankofa Market is Ghana's leading online marketplace where individuals and businesses can buy and sell a wide range of products. Think of it as a modern-day digital market where sellers list their items and buyers can browse, compare, and purchase from the comfort of their homes. We support multiple payment methods including Mobile Money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo) and card payments through Paystack.

How do I create an account?

Creating an account is easy! Click the "Register" button on the top right of any page. You'll need to provide your name, email address, phone number, and create a password. For enhanced security and trust, we also require Ghana Card verification. Once verified, you can start buying and selling immediately.

Why do I need to verify my Ghana Card?

Ghana Card verification helps us ensure a safe and trustworthy marketplace. It confirms your identity, protects against fraud, and builds trust between buyers and sellers. Verified users get a blue checkmark badge on their profile, which makes other users more confident in transacting with them. Your personal data is encrypted and stored securely in compliance with our Privacy Policy.

Is it free to sell on Sankofa Market?

Yes, listing products on Sankofa Market is completely free! We charge a small commission only when you make a successful sale. Free Starter: 5% commission on every sale. Premium: only 1–2% — Bronze 2%, Silver 1.5%, Gold 1.2%, Diamond 1%. Premium also gives you more listings (30 up to unlimited) + up to 10× reach. There are no hidden fees — see Premium plans.

Buying

How do I make a purchase?

Browse products, click "Add to Cart" for items you want, then proceed to checkout. You can review your cart, choose delivery options, and make payment. We support Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo Money), credit/debit cards via Paystack, and bank transfers. Your payment is held securely until you confirm delivery of your item.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept the following payment methods:

  • Mobile Money: MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, AirtelTigo Money
  • Cards: Visa, Mastercard, and other cards via Paystack
  • Bank Transfer: Direct transfer to our escrow account

All payments are processed securely, and your funds are protected by our escrow system until delivery is confirmed.

How does delivery work?

Delivery options vary by seller and location. Most sellers in Accra offer same-day or next-day delivery. For deliveries outside Accra, expect 2-5 business days. You can track your order in real-time from your "My Orders" page. Some sellers also offer pick-up points if you prefer to collect the item yourself.

What if I receive a damaged or wrong item?

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, don't confirm delivery! Instead, open a dispute within 48 hours from your "My Orders" page. Upload photos of the item and describe the issue. Our team will review your case and facilitate a return or refund. Your money is protected by our escrow system, so you'll never lose money on a fraudulent transaction.

What is the refund policy?

You can request a refund within 7 days of delivery if the item is damaged, not as described, or not received. Refunds are processed to your original payment method within 5-10 business days. Please see our Refund Policy for complete details.

Selling

How do I list a product for sale?

Click the "Sell" button on any page to start listing a product. You'll need to provide a title, description, price, category, photos (at least 3 recommended), and your location. Make sure your photos are clear and your description is honest and detailed. Good listings get more views and sales!

How much commission does Sankofa Market charge?

Free Starter — 5% on every successful sale (max 2 active listings).

Premium — only 1–2%:

  • 🥉 Bronze (2× Reach): 2% — GHS 45/mo
  • 🥈 Silver (4× Reach): 1.5% — GHS 79/mo
  • 🥇 Gold (6× Reach): 1.2% — GHS 129/mo
  • 💎 Diamond (10× Reach): 1% — GHS 199/mo

Commission is deducted from the sale price before payout. Listing is always free — you only pay when you sell, and Premium keeps your fees at 1–2%! Compare Premium plans

How do I get paid for my sales?

Once a buyer confirms delivery, funds are released to your Sankofa Market wallet. You can then withdraw to your Mobile Money wallet or bank account. Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours. You can set up your preferred payout method in your profile settings.

What items are prohibited from being sold?

Prohibited items include illegal substances, weapons, counterfeit goods, stolen property, live animals, prescription medications without a license, and any items that violate Ghanaian law. Please see our Prohibited Items Policy for the complete list. Violations may result in account suspension or permanent ban.

Account & Security

How do I keep my account safe?

Follow these security best practices:

  • Use a strong, unique password (at least 8 characters with letters, numbers, and symbols)
  • Never share your password or OTP codes with anyone
  • Enable two-factor authentication in your profile settings
  • Always complete transactions within Sankofa Market — never pay outside the platform
  • Report suspicious users or listings immediately
I forgot my password. How do I reset it?

Click "Forgot Password?" on the login page. Enter your registered email address, and we'll send you a reset link. The link expires after 30 minutes for security. If you don't receive the email, check your spam folder or contact our support team.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from your Profile page under the "Danger Zone" section. Please note that account deletion is permanent and all your data, listings, and order history will be permanently removed. If you have active orders or pending payouts, please resolve those before deleting your account. You can also choose to temporarily deactivate your account instead.

Sankofa Store (Official Store)

What is the Sankofa Store?

The Sankofa Store is our official in-house store where we sell curated, quality-verified products directly to our customers. Products from the Sankofa Store come with our quality guarantee, faster shipping, and dedicated customer support. We feature authentic Ghanaian products including traditional textiles, handicrafts, beauty products, and more.

Can I sell my products through the Sankofa Store?

Yes! If you're a producer or artisan with quality Ghanaian products, you can apply to sell through the Sankofa Store. We look for authentic, high-quality products that represent Ghanaian culture and craftsmanship. Contact our team at sankofamarketgh@gmail.com.gh with details about your products, and we'll review your application.

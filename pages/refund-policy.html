Refund Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

Overview

Sankofa Market facilitates transactions between buyers and sellers. Our refund policy applies ONLY to transactions completed through our Platform's escrow system.

IMPORTANT: Payments made outside our Platform (direct Mobile Money, bank transfers, cash) are NOT eligible for refunds through Sankofa Market. Such transactions are at your own risk.

1. Eligibility for Refunds

1.1 Qualifying Conditions

You may request a refund if:

  • Item received is significantly different from the listing description
  • Item is defective, damaged, or non-functional
  • Wrong item was delivered
  • Item was not delivered within the agreed timeframe
  • Item is counterfeit or infringes intellectual property rights

1.2 Non-Qualifying Conditions

Refunds are NOT available for:

  • Change of mind or buyer's remorse
  • Items that match the description but don't meet expectations
  • Items damaged by buyer after delivery
  • Disputes raised after 48-hour inspection period
  • Payments made outside the Platform
  • Services already rendered
  • Digital goods already accessed

2. Refund Process

2.1 Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Inspect Item: Upon delivery, inspect the item within 48 hours
  2. Contact Seller: First, try to resolve the issue directly with the seller
  3. Raise Dispute: If unresolved, raise a dispute through your order details page
  4. Provide Evidence: Upload photos, videos, and detailed descriptions of the issue
  5. Admin Review: Our team reviews the dispute within 3-5 business days
  6. Decision: Admin makes a decision based on evidence provided
  7. Refund Processing: If approved, refund is processed within 5-7 business days

2.2 Timeframes

  • Inspection Period: 48 hours from delivery confirmation
  • Dispute Filing: Must be filed within inspection period
  • Admin Review: 3-5 business days
  • Refund Processing: 5-7 business days after approval

3. Refund Amounts

3.1 Full Refunds

Full refunds (100% of purchase price) are issued when:

  • Item is significantly not as described
  • Item is defective or non-functional
  • Wrong item delivered
  • Item not delivered

3.2 Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may be issued when:

  • Item has minor issues but is still usable
  • Both parties agree to a partial refund
  • Item is returned in used condition

3.3 Non-Refundable Amounts

The following are typically non-refundable:

  • Delivery fees (unless delivery failed)
  • Service fees
  • Return shipping costs (unless seller agrees)

4. Return Shipping

4.1 Who Pays for Return Shipping?

  • Seller pays: If item is defective, wrong, or significantly not as described
  • Buyer pays: If returning due to change of mind (if seller accepts returns)
  • Shared: May be split between parties in partial refund scenarios

4.2 Return Process

  1. Admin approves refund with return requirement
  2. Buyer ships item back to seller with tracking
  3. Seller confirms receipt of returned item
  4. Refund is processed after confirmation

5. Refund Methods

Refunds are processed back to the original payment method:

  • Mobile Money: Refunded to same MoMo number within 5-7 days
  • Credit/Debit Card: Refunded to same card within 7-10 days
  • Bank Transfer: Refunded to same account within 7-10 days

Processing times depend on your payment provider and may vary.

6. Dispute Resolution

6.1 Admin Decision Factors

Our admin team considers:

  • Listing description and photos
  • Communication between buyer and seller
  • Photo/video evidence provided
  • Delivery confirmation and tracking
  • Seller's history and ratings
  • Applicable laws and regulations

6.2 Possible Outcomes

  • Full Refund: Buyer receives full refund, seller gets item back
  • Partial Refund: Buyer keeps item, receives partial refund
  • No Refund: Payment released to seller, buyer keeps item
  • Return Required: Buyer must return item for refund

6.3 Appeals

If you disagree with the admin decision, you may appeal within 7 days by providing additional evidence. Appeals are reviewed by senior admin staff.

7. Seller Protection

Sellers are protected from fraudulent refund claims:

  • Buyers must provide evidence for refund claims
  • Admin reviews all disputes impartially
  • Sellers can provide counter-evidence
  • Repeat fraudulent buyers may be banned

8. Limitations

8.1 Platform Limitations

Sankofa Market is NOT responsible for:

  • Refunds for payments made outside the Platform
  • Disputes filed after 48-hour inspection period
  • Subjective preferences (color, style, fit)
  • Items damaged by buyer after delivery
  • Transactions between users who bypass Platform

8.2 Maximum Liability

Our maximum liability for any refund claim is limited to the purchase price of the item (excluding delivery fees).

9. Fraud Prevention

We actively monitor for refund fraud:

  • Pattern analysis of refund requests
  • Verification of evidence provided
  • Review of user history and behavior
  • Collaboration with payment providers

Users found engaging in refund fraud will have their accounts suspended and may face legal action.

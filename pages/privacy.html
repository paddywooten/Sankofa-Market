Introduction

Sankofa Market ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our marketplace platform.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By accessing or using the Platform, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you:

  • Register an account: Ghana Card number, name, email, phone number, password
  • Create listings: Item descriptions, photos, pricing, location
  • Make purchases: Delivery address, payment information
  • Contact support: Messages, inquiries, feedback
  • Participate in promotions: Contest entries, survey responses

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

When you use our Platform, we automatically collect:

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent, click patterns, search queries
  • Location Data: General location based on IP address (with your consent for precise location)
  • Cookies and Tracking Technologies: See our Cookie Policy for details
  • Transaction Data: Purchase history, payment methods, amounts

1.3 Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from:

  • Payment processors (Paystack, Mobile Money providers)
  • Social media platforms (if you link your accounts)
  • Identity verification services
  • Other users (reviews, ratings, messages)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

2.1 Platform Operations

  • Create and manage your account
  • Process transactions and payments
  • Facilitate communication between buyers and sellers
  • Provide customer support
  • Verify your identity (Ghana Card verification)

2.2 Platform Improvement

  • Analyze usage patterns to improve user experience
  • Develop new features and services
  • Personalize your experience (recommendations, search results)
  • Conduct research and analysis

2.3 Safety and Security

  • Prevent fraud and illegal activities
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Resolve disputes between users
  • Protect our rights and users' safety
  • Comply with legal obligations

2.4 Marketing and Communications

  • Send promotional emails (with your consent)
  • Notify you about Platform updates
  • Send transaction-related communications
  • Respond to your inquiries

3. Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell your personal information. We share your information only in the following circumstances:

3.1 With Other Users

  • Sellers: Your delivery address and contact information when you make a purchase
  • Buyers: Your seller profile, ratings, and item listings
  • All Users: Your public profile information (name, ratings, reviews)

3.2 With Service Providers

We share information with third-party service providers who assist us in:

  • Payment processing (Paystack, Mobile Money providers)
  • Identity verification
  • Cloud hosting and storage (Firebase, Google Cloud)
  • Email and SMS delivery
  • Analytics and monitoring
  • Customer support

These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the services they provide to us.

3.3 For Legal Reasons

We may disclose your information if required to:

  • Comply with legal obligations (court orders, subpoenas)
  • Protect our rights and users' safety
  • Prevent fraud or illegal activities
  • Enforce our Terms of Service
  • Respond to law enforcement requests

3.4 Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the acquiring entity. We will notify you of such changes.

3.5 With Your Consent

We may share your information with third parties when you give us explicit consent to do so.

4. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

4.1 Technical Safeguards

  • Encryption: SSL/TLS encryption for data in transit
  • Secure Storage: Encrypted databases and secure cloud storage
  • Access Controls: Role-based access and authentication
  • Firewalls: Network security and intrusion detection
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and penetration testing

4.2 Organizational Safeguards

  • Employee training on data protection
  • Confidentiality agreements with staff
  • Limited access to personal information
  • Incident response procedures

4.3 Your Responsibilities

You also play a role in protecting your information:

  • Choose a strong, unique password
  • Do not share your account credentials
  • Log out when using shared devices
  • Be cautious of phishing attempts
  • Report suspicious activities immediately

Important: While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy:

  • Account Information: Retained while your account is active and for 30 days after deletion request
  • Transaction Data: Retained for 7 years for legal and tax purposes
  • Communication Records: Retained for 2 years for dispute resolution
  • Usage Data: Retained for 2 years for analytics and improvement
  • Chatbot Conversations: Retained for 90 days (configurable)

We may retain anonymized or aggregated data for research and analytics purposes.

6.4 Objection and Restriction

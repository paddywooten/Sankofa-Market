Acceptable Use Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

Purpose

This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") outlines the rules and guidelines for using Sankofa Market. By accessing or using our Platform, you agree to comply with this AUP. Violations may result in account suspension or termination.

1. General Conduct

1.1 Respectful Behavior

All users must treat others with respect and courtesy. Prohibited behaviors include:

  • Harassment, bullying, or intimidation of other users
  • Hate speech or discriminatory language
  • Threats of violence or harm
  • Defamatory or libelous statements
  • Invasion of privacy

1.2 Honesty and Integrity

Users must act honestly and with integrity in all Platform interactions:

  • Do not misrepresent yourself, your items, or your services
  • Do not engage in fraudulent or deceptive practices
  • Do not manipulate reviews, ratings, or feedback
  • Do not create fake accounts or multiple accounts to circumvent restrictions

2. Account Usage

2.1 Account Security

You are responsible for:

  • Maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials
  • All activities that occur under your account
  • Notifying us immediately of any unauthorized access
  • Using strong, unique passwords
  • Not sharing your account with others

2.2 Account Eligibility

  • You must be at least 18 years old to create an account
  • You must provide accurate, truthful information during registration
  • You must verify your identity with a valid Ghana Card
  • You must not create multiple accounts without permission

2.3 Account Transfer

Accounts are non-transferable. You may not sell, trade, or transfer your account to another person without our explicit written permission.

3. Listing and Selling

3.1 Accurate Listings

All listings must:

  • Accurately describe the item or service
  • Include clear, truthful photographs
  • State the correct price and condition
  • Disclose any defects, damage, or issues
  • Comply with our Prohibited Items Policy

3.2 Prohibited Listing Practices

You may NOT:

  • List items you do not own or have the right to sell
  • Use stock photos without disclosing they are not actual item photos
  • Create duplicate listings for the same item
  • Use misleading titles or descriptions to attract attention
  • Manipulate search results through keyword stuffing
  • Offer items at artificially low prices to attract buyers, then claim they're unavailable

3.3 Pricing and Fees

  • Prices must be stated in Ghana Cedis (GHS)
  • All fees and commissions must be clearly disclosed
  • Do not attempt to circumvent Platform fees
  • Do not offer discounts for off-Platform transactions

4. Buying and Transactions

4.1 Payment Methods

CRITICAL: Only use Platform payment methods. Sankofa Market is NOT responsible for disputes arising from payments made outside the Platform.

  • Use only the Platform's escrow system for payments
  • Do NOT make direct payments to sellers
  • Do NOT use external payment services (PayPal, Western Union, etc.)
  • Do NOT pay via direct Mobile Money or bank transfer

4.2 Transaction Conduct

  • Honor your commitments (complete purchases you initiate)
  • Inspect items promptly upon delivery (within 48 hours)
  • Raise disputes within the inspection period if issues arise
  • Do not abuse the dispute system
  • Provide honest feedback and reviews

5. Communication

5.1 Platform Messaging

  • Use Platform messaging for all transaction-related communication
  • Do not share personal contact information (phone, email) in messages
  • Do not use messaging to harass, spam, or abuse others
  • Do not send unsolicited commercial messages (spam)

5.2 Prohibited Communication

You may NOT:

  • Send threatening, abusive, or offensive messages
  • Engage in phishing or social engineering attacks
  • Impersonate Sankofa Market staff or other users
  • Send malware or malicious links
  • Circumvent Platform communication systems

6. Reviews and Feedback

6.1 Honest Reviews

Reviews and feedback must be:

  • Based on actual transactions or interactions
  • Honest and truthful
  • Relevant to the transaction
  • Free from profanity, hate speech, or personal attacks

6.2 Prohibited Review Practices

You may NOT:

  • Post fake reviews (positive or negative)
  • Offer incentives for positive reviews
  • Threaten negative reviews to extort concessions
  • Post reviews for transactions you were not involved in
  • Use reviews to post personal information or advertisements

7. Intellectual Property

7.1 Respect for IP Rights

  • Only list items you have the right to sell
  • Do not infringe on trademarks, copyrights, or patents
  • Do not use others' photos or content without permission
  • Do not sell counterfeit or pirated goods

7.2 User Content License

By posting content on the Platform, you grant Sankofa Market a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use, display, and distribute your content for Platform operations.

8. Platform Integrity

8.1 Technical Abuse

You may NOT:

  • Use automated systems or bots without permission
  • Scrape or harvest data from the Platform
  • Attempt to gain unauthorized access to Platform systems
  • Interfere with the proper functioning of the Platform
  • Distribute malware or viruses
  • Use the Platform for cryptocurrency mining

8.2 Manipulation and Fraud

You may NOT:

  • Create fake accounts to manipulate ratings or reviews
  • Engage in shill bidding or price manipulation
  • Use multiple accounts to circumvent restrictions
  • Engage in any form of fraud or deception

9. Legal Compliance

9.1 Compliance with Laws

You must comply with all applicable laws and regulations, including:

  • Ghanaian laws and regulations
  • International trade laws (for cross-border transactions)
  • Tax laws and reporting requirements
  • Consumer protection laws
  • Data protection and privacy laws

9.2 Prohibited Activities

You may NOT use the Platform for:

  • Money laundering or terrorist financing
  • Tax evasion
  • Illegal gambling
  • Securities fraud
  • Any other illegal activity

10. Enforcement

10.1 Monitoring

Sankofa Market monitors Platform usage to ensure compliance with this AUP. We may:

  • Review listings, messages, and transactions
  • Investigate reported violations
  • Use automated systems to detect violations
  • Cooperate with law enforcement when required

10.2 Penalties

Violations of this AUP may result in:

  • Warning: For minor, first-time violations
  • Listing Removal: Removal of violating content
  • Account Suspension: Temporary suspension (1-30 days)
  • Account Termination: Permanent ban from Platform
  • Legal Action: For serious violations or illegal activities

10.3 Appeals

If you believe a penalty was applied in error:

  1. Email sankofamarketgh@gmail.com within 7 days
  2. Include your account information and explanation
  3. Provide any supporting evidence
  4. Our team will review within 5 business days

11. Reporting Violations

If you encounter violations of this AUP:

  • Use the "Report" button on listings or profiles
  • Email: sankofamarketgh@gmail.com
  • Provide detailed information about the violation
  • Include screenshots or evidence if available

We take all reports seriously and will investigate promptly.

12. Policy Updates

This Acceptable Use Policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

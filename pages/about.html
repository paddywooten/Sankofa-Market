About Sankofa Market

Ghana's Trusted Online Marketplace

Our Story

Sankofa Market was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to create a safe, trustworthy, and accessible online marketplace for Ghanaians to buy and sell goods and services.

The name "Sankofa" comes from the Akan language of Ghana, meaning "to go back and get it." It represents the wisdom of learning from the past while moving forward—a philosophy that guides our approach to building a modern marketplace that respects traditional values of trust, community, and fair trade.

Launched in 2026, Sankofa Market has quickly become Ghana's premier C2C (consumer-to-consumer) marketplace, connecting thousands of buyers and sellers across the country.

Our Mission

To empower Ghanaians by providing a secure, user-friendly platform where anyone can buy and sell with confidence, while fostering economic growth and entrepreneurship across Ghana.

What Makes Us Different

Ghana Card Verification

All users must verify their identity with their Ghana Card, ensuring a trusted community of real people.

Escrow Payment Protection

Our escrow system holds payments securely until buyers confirm delivery, protecting both parties.

Admin Dispute Resolution

Our dedicated admin team reviews disputes fairly and makes binding decisions based on evidence.

Mobile Money Integration

Seamless payments via MTN, Vodafone, and AirtelTigo Mobile Money, plus card payments.

Flexible Delivery Options

Sellers can offer free delivery, paid delivery, or pickup-only options to suit their capabilities.

AI-Powered Chatbot

24/7 intelligent assistant helps users with FAQs, smart suggestions, and human escalation.

Visual Search

Upload images to find similar products using advanced image recognition technology.

Our Commitment to Ghana

Sankofa Market is more than just a marketplace—we are a Ghanaian company committed to contributing to our country's economic development:

  • Empowering Entrepreneurs: We provide a platform for small businesses and individuals to reach customers across Ghana, fostering entrepreneurship and economic growth.
  • Creating Jobs: We employ Ghanaians across various departments, from technology to customer support.
  • Supporting Local Economy: By facilitating local transactions, we help keep money circulating within the Ghanaian economy.
  • Promoting Digital Inclusion: We make e-commerce accessible to Ghanaians from all walks of life, regardless of technical expertise.
  • Building Trust in E-Commerce: Through our verification and protection systems, we help build confidence in online transactions.

Our Technology

Sankofa Market is built on modern, scalable technology to ensure reliability, security, and performance:

  • Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (ES6+), responsive design
  • Backend: Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Storage, Cloud Functions)
  • Payments: Paystack integration for Mobile Money and card payments
  • AI/ML: Google Cloud Vision API for image recognition and NLP
  • Security: SSL encryption, Firebase security rules, admin authentication
  • Hosting: Firebase Hosting with global CDN

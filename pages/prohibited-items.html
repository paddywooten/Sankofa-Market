Prohibited Items Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

Overview

To ensure the safety of our users and compliance with Ghanaian laws, Sankofa Market prohibits the listing of certain items. This policy outlines items that are NOT allowed on our platform.

Important: Listing prohibited items may result in immediate removal of the listing, account suspension, or permanent ban. Repeat offenders may face legal action.

1. Illegal Items

Items that are illegal under Ghanaian law or international law are strictly prohibited:

  • Drugs and Narcotics: Illegal drugs, controlled substances, drug paraphernalia
  • Weapons: Firearms, ammunition, explosives, knives (except kitchen knives)
  • Stolen Goods: Items obtained through theft or illegal means
  • Counterfeit Items: Fake goods that infringe on trademarks or copyrights
  • Illegal Wildlife Products: Ivory, endangered species products, protected plants
  • Hazardous Materials: Toxic chemicals, radioactive materials, biohazards
  • Illegal Services: Prostitution, illegal gambling, money laundering services

2. Dangerous Items

Items that pose safety risks to users or the public:

  • Recalled Products: Items subject to manufacturer recalls
  • Defective Items: Products with known safety defects
  • Expired Products: Food, medicine, cosmetics past expiration date
  • Unsafe Electronics: Products without proper safety certifications
  • Fireworks: All types of fireworks and pyrotechnics
  • Flammable Items: Gasoline, propane tanks, lighter fluid (except small quantities for household use)

3. Offensive and Harmful Content

Items that are offensive, discriminatory, or promote harm:

  • Hate Symbols: Nazi memorabilia, KKK items, racist propaganda
  • Discriminatory Items: Items that promote discrimination based on race, religion, gender, sexual orientation, or disability
  • Violent Content: Items promoting violence, terrorism, or self-harm
  • Adult Content: Pornography, explicit sexual materials, adult services
  • Offensive Items: Items that are grossly offensive to community standards

4. Regulated Items (Requires Special Permission)

Items that require special licenses, permits, or verification:

  • Prescription Medications: Only licensed pharmacies may list (with verification)
  • Medical Devices: Requires proper certifications and licenses
  • Alcohol: Only licensed vendors with age verification systems
  • Tobacco Products: Only licensed vendors with age verification
  • Pesticides: Requires proper licensing and safety information
  • Real Estate: Only verified agents and property owners
  • Vehicles: Requires proper documentation and ownership proof

Note: If you believe you qualify to list regulated items, contact our compliance team at compliance@sankofamarket.com with proper documentation.

5. Intellectual Property Violations

Items that infringe on intellectual property rights:

  • Counterfeit Goods: Fake designer items, bootleg merchandise
  • Unauthorized Copies: Pirated software, movies, music, books
  • Trademark Infringement: Items using trademarks without permission
  • Copyright Infringement: Unauthorized reproductions of copyrighted works
  • Patent Infringement: Items that violate patent rights

6. Personal Information and Services

Items and services involving personal data or identity:

  • Personal Data: Selling personal information, email lists, phone numbers
  • Identity Documents: Fake IDs, passports, driver's licenses
  • Academic Credentials: Fake diplomas, certificates, transcripts
  • Government Documents: Fake government-issued documents
  • Hacking Services: Services to hack accounts or systems
  • Surveillance Equipment: Hidden cameras, spyware (except for legitimate security use)

7. Financial Instruments

Items related to financial fraud or unauthorized financial services:

  • Credit Cards: Stolen or unauthorized credit/debit cards
  • Bank Account Information: Selling bank account details
  • Investment Schemes: Ponzi schemes, pyramid schemes, fraudulent investments
  • Currency: Counterfeit currency, unauthorized currency exchange
  • Lottery Tickets: Unauthorized lottery or gambling tickets

8. Services Prohibited on Platform

Services that cannot be offered through Sankofa Market:

  • Illegal Services: Any service that violates Ghanaian law
  • Adult Services: Escort services, adult entertainment
  • Medical Services: Unlicensed medical advice or treatment
  • Legal Services: Unlicensed legal advice or representation
  • Financial Advice: Unlicensed financial or investment advice
  • Multi-Level Marketing: MLM schemes, pyramid structures

9. Animals and Wildlife

Items related to animals and wildlife:

  • Endangered Species: Live endangered animals or their products
  • Illegal Wildlife Products: Ivory, rhino horn, pangolin scales
  • Live Animals: Most live animals (except fish, small pets with proper documentation)
  • Animal Parts: Parts from protected or endangered species
  • Animal Fighting: Equipment for animal fighting

Allowed: Pet supplies, pet food, aquarium equipment, and domestic pets with proper health certificates.

10. Miscellaneous Prohibited Items

Other items not allowed on the platform:

  • Used Underwear: Used intimate apparel
  • Used Cosmetics: Used makeup, skincare products (hygiene concerns)
  • Event Tickets: Scalped or unauthorized resale of event tickets
  • Gift Cards: Unless from verified sellers with proof of purchase
  • Digital Accounts: Selling social media accounts, gaming accounts
  • Surplus Government Property: Unauthorized sale of government property
  • Human Remains: Any human body parts or remains
  • Artifacts: Cultural artifacts, archaeological items without proper documentation

Enforcement

10.1 Monitoring and Detection

We use automated systems and manual review to detect prohibited items:

  • Automated keyword and image detection
  • User reports and complaints
  • Random listing audits
  • Pattern analysis for suspicious activity

10.2 Penalties for Violations

Consequences for listing prohibited items:

  • First Offense: Listing removed, warning issued
  • Second Offense: Listing removed, 7-day account suspension
  • Third Offense: Permanent account ban
  • Serious Violations: Immediate permanent ban, possible legal action

10.3 Reporting Violations

If you encounter a prohibited item, please report it:

  • Click the "Report" button on the listing
  • Email: violations@sankofamarket.com
  • Provide listing ID and reason for report

Appeals Process

If you believe your listing was removed in error:

  1. Email appeals@sankofamarket.com within 7 days of removal
  2. Include listing ID and explanation
  3. Provide any supporting documentation
  4. Our team will review within 5 business days
  5. Decision will be communicated via email

Policy Updates

This Prohibited Items Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in laws, regulations, or platform policies. Users are responsible for reviewing this policy periodically.

Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Questions?

If you're unsure whether an item is allowed on Sankofa Market:

  • Email: compliance@sankofamarket.com
  • Subject: "Item Eligibility Inquiry - [Item Description]"
  • Include: Item description, photos, and any relevant documentation

We will respond within 48 hours with guidance.

Remember: When in doubt, don't list it. It's better to ask first than to risk account suspension or legal consequences.

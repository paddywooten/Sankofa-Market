Seller Guide

Everything you need to know to start selling on Ghana's #1 online marketplace. From creating your first listing to getting paid — we've got you covered.

Getting Started

1

Create & Verify Your Account

Sign up with your email and phone number, then verify your identity with your Ghana Card. Verified sellers get a trust badge that boosts buyer confidence and increases sales. Verification takes less than 24 hours.

2

Complete Your Seller Profile

Add a professional profile photo, write a compelling bio, and include your business details. Set up your payout method (Mobile Money or bank account) so you can receive payments as soon as your first sale comes in.

3

Create Your First Listing

Click "Sell" to start listing. Write a clear, descriptive title, add at least 3 high-quality photos from different angles, write a detailed description, set a competitive price, and choose the right category. Good listings sell faster!

4

Manage Orders & Get Paid

When a buyer places an order, you'll receive a notification. Package and ship the item promptly. Once the buyer confirms delivery, funds are released to your wallet. Withdraw anytime to your Mobile Money or bank account.

Pro Tips for Successful Selling

Take Great Photos

Use natural lighting, shoot against a clean background, and show the product from multiple angles. Listings with 5+ photos get 3x more views. Avoid blurry or dark images.

Price Competitively

Research similar products on Sankofa Market to price your items competitively. Consider offering bundle discounts or free delivery for orders above a certain amount to attract more buyers.

Write Compelling Descriptions

Be honest and detailed. Include dimensions, materials, condition (new/used), color options, and any special features. Mention the story behind handmade products — buyers love that!

Respond Quickly

Reply to buyer messages within a few hours. Fast responders get a "Quick Responder" badge and are more likely to close sales. Set up message notifications on your phone.

Ship Promptly

Ship orders within 24 hours of receiving them. Use reliable delivery services and provide tracking information. Fast shipping leads to 5-star reviews and repeat customers.

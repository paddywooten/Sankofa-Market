1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide useful information to website owners.

Cookies allow websites to:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Keep you logged in to your account
  • Understand how you use the website
  • Provide personalized content and advertisements
  • Improve website performance and user experience

2. How We Use Cookies

Sankofa Market uses cookies for various purposes to enhance your experience on our platform:

2.1 Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as:

  • User authentication and session management
  • Shopping cart functionality
  • Security features
  • Load balancing

Note: These cookies are essential and cannot be disabled. Without them, the website would not work properly.

2.2 Performance and Analytics Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting and reporting information anonymously:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Links clicked
  • Error messages encountered
  • Browser and device information

Purpose: To improve website performance, fix issues, and understand user behavior.

2.3 Functionality Cookies

These cookies enable enhanced functionality and personalization:

  • Remembering your preferences (language, region, currency)
  • Saving your search filters
  • Remembering items in your wishlist
  • Providing personalized recommendations

Purpose: To provide a more personalized and convenient experience.

2.4 Advertising and Marketing Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver relevant advertisements and track campaign performance:

  • Showing ads based on your interests
  • Limiting the number of times you see an ad
  • Measuring advertising campaign effectiveness
  • Retargeting (showing ads for products you've viewed)

Purpose: To show you relevant advertisements and measure marketing effectiveness.

4. Managing Cookies

4.1 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • View and delete existing cookies
  • Block all cookies or only third-party cookies
  • Set preferences for specific websites
  • Delete cookies when you close your browser

How to Manage Cookies in Popular Browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and site data

4.2 Cookie Consent

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner that allows you to:

  • Accept All: Allow all cookies (essential, analytics, marketing)
  • Accept Essential Only: Allow only essential cookies
  • Customize: Choose which types of cookies to allow

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer of our website.

4.3 Opt-Out Options

You can opt out of certain types of cookies:

  • Google Analytics: Opt out here
  • Facebook Pixel: Adjust your Facebook ad preferences
  • Google Ads: Adjust ad settings

5. Impact of Disabling Cookies

If you choose to disable cookies, some features of our website may not function properly:

5.1 Essential Cookies Disabled

  • You won't be able to log in to your account
  • Shopping cart won't work
  • You won't be able to make purchases
  • Some security features may be compromised

Recommendation: Do not disable essential cookies as the website will not function properly.

5.2 Functionality Cookies Disabled

  • Your preferences won't be saved
  • You'll need to re-enter search filters each time
  • Wishlist may not persist
  • Personalized recommendations won't be available

5.3 Analytics Cookies Disabled

  • Your visits won't be tracked in our analytics
  • This doesn't affect your browsing experience
  • Helps protect your privacy

5.4 Marketing Cookies Disabled

  • You'll see generic ads instead of personalized ones
  • You may see the same ads repeatedly
  • Ads may be less relevant to your interests

6. Cookie Duration

Cookies have different lifespans depending on their purpose:

6.1 Session Cookies

These are temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser:

  • Session ID cookies
  • Temporary authentication tokens
  • Shopping cart session data

6.2 Persistent Cookies

These remain on your device for a specified period:

  • Short-term (1-30 days): Search filters, temporary preferences
  • Medium-term (30-180 days): User preferences, wishlist items
  • Long-term (180+ days): Authentication tokens, long-term preferences

Business Setup
✓
Register Company at Registrar General's Department
Register as a Limited Liability Company or Sole Proprietorship. You'll receive a Certificate of Incorporation and Certificate to Commence Business.
Required RGD Office or online GH₵ 500–1,500 1–2 weeks
✓
Get Taxpayer Identification Number (TIN)
Register with the Ghana Revenue Authority for your TIN. Required for all tax filings and business transactions.
Required GRA / jtb.gov.gh/tin-registration Free 1–3 days
✓
Open Business Bank Account
Open a corporate bank account in your company name. Required for receiving payments, Paystack settlement, and tax payments.
Required Any commercial bank Varies 1 week
Data Protection & Privacy
✓
Register with Data Protection Commission
Since you collect user data (names, emails, phone numbers, Ghana Card numbers), you must register as a Data Controller. Annual renewal required. Penalty: up to GH₵ 60,000 fine.
Required dataprotection.org.gh GH₵ 500–2,000/year 1–2 weeks
✓
Appoint a Data Protection Supervisor
Designate someone responsible for ensuring data protection compliance. Can be yourself as the business owner.
Required Same day
Payment Processing
✓
Set Up Paystack Business Account
Complete business verification on Paystack (not personal). Requires company registration docs, Ghana Card, and business bank account. Enables card payments.
Required paystack.com Free (commission per txn) 1 week
✓
Register Mobile Money Merchant Account
Register as a merchant with MTN MoMo, Vodafone Cash, and/or AirtelTigo Money to accept mobile money payments from customers.
Recommended MTN / Vodafone / AirtelTigo Varies 1–2 weeks
E-Commerce Compliance
✓
Register with PCSRC (Postal & Courier Regulatory Commission)
Government directive requires all e-commerce platforms and their delivery drivers to register. Ensures accountability and prevents delivery fraud.
Required (if delivering) PCSRC office Varies 2–4 weeks
✓
Electronic Transactions Act (Act 772) Compliance
Ensure your platform provides: clear seller info, accurate pricing, refund policy, terms of service, privacy policy, dispute resolution, and keeps electronic records. Most of these are already on your website ✅
Required Your website Already done ✅
Tax Obligations
✓
Register for Corporate Income Tax
25% tax on net profits. File annual returns with GRA. Keep proper financial records of all transactions, commissions, and expenses.
Required GRA office 25% of profits
✓
Register for VAT (if turnover exceeds GH₵ 250,000/year)
15% VAT on services. Required once annual revenue exceeds the threshold. File monthly VAT returns.
When you grow GRA office 15% on services
Recommended Protection
✓
Implement Escrow Payment System
Hold buyer payments until delivery is confirmed. Protects both buyers and sellers. Builds trust and reduces fraud disputes.
Recommended Your platform
Note: This checklist is based on Ghana's Electronic Transactions Act 2008 (Act 772), Companies Act 2019 (Act 992), Data Protection Act 2012 (Act 843), and Payment Systems & Services Act 2019 (Act 987). Requirements may change — consult a lawyer for up-to-date legal advice. Your progress is saved automatically.

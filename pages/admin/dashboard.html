🏆 Founding Seller Program

  • Offer: 0% commission for the first 50–100 sellers for 3 months
  • Extras: "Founding Seller" badge + featured homepage placement
  • How to enroll: Users tab → click the 🏆 button next to the seller
  • Pitch: "Be one of Ghana's first trusted sellers — keep 100% of your sales for 3 months"

Where to Find Sellers (Recruitment Playbook)

📱 Instagram & WhatsApp Sellers

They already have inventory and photos. Search hashtags like #AccraVendors, #GhanaSellers, #KumasiBusiness.

Pitch: "Your own storefront + buyers pay safely via escrow. No more payment disputes."

🎓 University Campuses

Legon, KNUST, UPSA, UCC, UMaT. Students constantly sell phones, books, fashion and furniture.

Pitch: "Campus Seller" campaign — 0% commission for their first month + campus ambassador program.

🏪 Market Vendors Going Digital

Kantamanto, Makola, Kejetia, Madina market. Visit stalls and onboard vendors in person.

Pitch: Physically help 5–10 vendors list their first items. One phone can onboard a whole stall. Use Direct Verify to start them instantly.

🎵 TikTok & Content Creators

Ghanaian creators with small businesses (fashion, food, crafts) looking for a sales channel.

Pitch: Free verification + featured placement in exchange for a shout-out video.

🤝 Referral Program

Verified sellers earn GHS 10 credit for every new seller they invite who completes a first sale.

Pitch: "Invite a friend to sell, earn credit toward your next purchase."

⛪ Community Groups

Church groups, associations, alumni networks — trust-based communities that already trade with each other.

Pitch: "Trade within your community, protected by escrow."

Verification Rules for Admins

  • Standard path: User submits Ghana Card number + passport photo → review within 24–48h → approve → they become 🆕 Starter (10 listings)
  • Direct Verify: Use for users you know personally, in-person onboarded market vendors, or trusted referrals. No documents needed — they start as Starter immediately.
  • Auto-promotion: A Starter who completes 2 escrow purchases is automatically promoted to ✅ Verified (200 listings).
  • Trusted promotion: After 10 completed escrow sales, promote to ⭐ Trusted (auto-detected; confirm manually via the tier dropdown).
  • Revoke: Use "Unverify" for policy violations, fraud reports, or repeated disputes. The reason is logged.
  • Escrow is mandatory for all tiers — it protects both buyers and sellers and keeps transactions on-platform.

Growth Metrics to Watch Weekly

  • % of new users making a first purchase within 7 days (if below ~20%, the escrow requirement needs softening)
  • Drop-off at each verification step (documents submitted → approved → first listing)
  • Starter → Verified conversion rate (target: 30% within 60 days)
  • Founding Sellers active vs. enrolled (target: 70%+ listing at least once)
  • New seller → first sale time (target: under 14 days)

Since you collect user data (names, emails, phone numbers, Ghana Card numbers), you must register as a Data Controller. Annual renewal required. Penalty: up to GHS 60,000 fine.
Required dataprotection.org.gh GHS 500–2,000/year 1–2 weeks

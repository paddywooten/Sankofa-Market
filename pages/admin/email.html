1. Your EmailJS details

These three values come from your own free EmailJS account. The key is stored on the server and is never sent to a browser — only its last few letters are shown below.

EmailJS → Email Services. The service must be connected to your Gmail.
EmailJS → Email Templates. This is the one template that shapes every email.
EmailJS → Account → General → Public Key. It is one word of about 18 letters and numbers. Leave this box empty if you only want to change the other boxes.
